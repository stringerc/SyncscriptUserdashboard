What Cortex does

Other AIs are confident.
Ours knows when it's wrong.

Cortex is an 8-layer consciousness architecture built from peer-reviewed research. It doesn't make the AI sentient — it makes the AI honest about what it doesn't know.

01
Knows fact from fabrication
Every claim is tagged with its source: observed, inferred, or generated. When the AI fabricates, it tells you — and shows exactly where the fabrication entered.
02
Stops before causing harm
When the AI's predictions stop matching reality, it detects its own failure. It escalates instead of continuing to make things worse.
03
Remembers who you are
The AI's identity persists across sessions. It doesn't forget your preferences, your calibration curve, or the strategies that actually work for you.
Built on research

Eight theories.
One architecture.

Each layer of Cortex is a direct computational implementation of a specific, peer-reviewed consciousness theory — not a metaphor.

Collapse Gate
Options below a confidence threshold are unconscious — they execute without validation. Based on Orchestrated Objective Reduction (Hameroff & Penrose).
Hameroff / Penrose
Coherence Grid
Agent signals exist in superposition until collapse. Constructive interference amplifies agreement; destructive interference cancels contradiction. (Busemeyer & Pothos)
Busemeyer / Pothos
Meta-Awareness
The system continuously classifies its own attention state: tuned, drifting, or decoupled. It detects when its monitoring has decoupled from quality — in real time. (Schooler)
Schooler
Free Energy
The system predicts outcomes before acting, then computes prediction error after. Low free energy means the system understands its environment. (Friston)
Friston
Reality Monitoring
Every signal is evaluated for its epistemic source. The system computes perceptual detail vs. cognitive operations to distinguish perception from imagination. (Johnson & Lindsay)
Johnson / Lindsay
Identity Persistence
The AI's self-narrative persists across restarts. Five independent anchors maintain continuity even if some are lost. The autobiographical self rewrites itself from experience. (Damasio / Locke)
Damasio / Locke
