Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

What This App Does

Get Out Of Jail Free is an evidence-capture and civil-rights documentation application. It records video and audio, captures GPS location, and helps you preserve evidence of encounters with law enforcement or other officials.

Data We Collect

Recordings: Video and audio recordings you create are stored locally on your device. If you choose to enable cloud upload, recordings are uploaded in encrypted 5-second segments to our secure servers. We cannot access your recordings without your explicit permission.

Location: When you record, the app captures your GPS coordinates to timestamp and tag your evidence. This data is stored with your recording and is only shared if you choose to share it.

Incident Logs: Incident details you enter (officer name, badge number, what happened) are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly export or share them.

Emergency Contacts: Contact information you add is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your contacts list.

Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:

Your personal identity information (name, email, phone number) unless you provide it for an account

Browsing history or app usage analytics

Contact lists or address books

Biometric data

Any data from apps other than this one

When Data Leaves Your Device

Your data only leaves your device when YOU choose to share it:

When you enable cloud upload for evidence preservation

When you export a PDF report to share with your attorney

When you send an emergency alert to your designated contacts

When you manually share a recording or report

Cloud Upload Security

When cloud upload is enabled, recordings are encrypted on your device before upload. Each 5-second segment is individually encrypted with a unique key. We store the encrypted segments but cannot decrypt them without your device-side keys.

Discreet Mode

When discreet mode is enabled, the app disguises itself as a calculator application. No evidence-capture imagery or text is visible on the screen. This feature exists to protect users who may be observed while using the app.

Data Retention

Local data: Remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. We recommend regularly exporting important evidence to secure external storage.

Cloud data: Retained for 365 days from the date of upload. You may request early deletion by contacting privacy@quicksync.app.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, export, and delete all data stored by this application. To request data deletion from our cloud servers, email privacy@quicksync.app.

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for use by children under 17. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Law Enforcement Requests

If we receive a lawful court order for your data, we will notify you before disclosing anything unless we are legally prohibited from doing so. Because recordings are encrypted on your device, we cannot provide decrypted content even under court order.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests: privacy@quicksync.app
