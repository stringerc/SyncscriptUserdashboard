# AI Listing Hook Pack

**50 scroll-stopping opening lines for real estate listings — organized by hook type, written to stop the scroll and create an open loop.**

## What's inside

- `hooks.md` — 50 ready-to-use listing hooks across 5 categories:
  - Lifestyle hooks (emotional pull)
  - Specificity hooks (concrete detail = credibility)
  - Curiosity hooks (open loops)
  - Neighborhood hooks (location as the product)
  - Proof hooks (social proof & credibility)
- Quick formulas section for when none of the 50 fit
- What-a-hook-is-NOT section (the anti-patterns that kill listings)

## How to use

1. Pick a hook matching the property + buyer type
2. Fill in [BRACKETS] with real details
3. Paste as the opening line of your listing description, social post, or email
4. Let the body of your listing close the loop

## What's deliberately NOT in here

This pack covers **opening lines only**. Full listing descriptions, the 100+ prompt library, Notion client pipeline, and Canva marketing suite live in the **Real Estate AI Listing Kit** — the natural next step once your hooks are working.
