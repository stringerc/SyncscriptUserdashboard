## FRAMING FOR LINGERIE / BED / LOCKED DOOR / SEX THERAPY

**Use questions, not accusations.** Questions can't be defamation.

SAFER: "I have been told that in the office where Katelyn conducts mental health evaluations, alone, behind a locked door, there is a bed. For a chiropractor who visits perhaps once a week or every other week. I'm going to let you decide whether that's appropriate for an APC conducting psychological assessments."

RISKIER (avoid): "Katelyn does evaluations in a sex room with a bed." — This is an assertion that could be challenged.
